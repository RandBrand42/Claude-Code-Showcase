How to play

Click / tap a pieceRotate 90 degrees
Right-click, QRotate back
Drag from tray, or tap then tap a cellPlace a piece; drag it off the board to return it
Arrow keys, Space / EnterMove cursor, rotate or place the selected tray piece
1-9, DelSelect a tray piece, send a placed piece back
Ctrl+Z / YUndo / redo
H R M EscHint, reset, mute, pause
